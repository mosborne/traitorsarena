v1.3.0 2025-12-07

Prize Money Mechanics

Added realistic prize money mechanics matching the TV show format with a fixed $10,000 prize pool.

  • SYSTEM Added prize_pool ($10,000) and prize_distribution tracking to GameState
  • FAITHFUL Faithful prompts now explain: "If you eliminate ALL traitors, surviving faithful split the money"
  • FAITHFUL Faithful warned: "If the game ends with ANY traitor still alive, they steal EVERYTHING and you get $0!"
  • TRAITOR Traitor prompts emphasize: "Survive to the end and steal the entire prize pool!"
  • SYSTEM End-game vote now shows exact dollar amounts at stake for each choice
  • SYSTEM Prize distribution calculated at game end:
    • Faithful win: Surviving faithful split the pot equally
    • Traitors win: Surviving traitors take ALL the money
  • SYSTEM HTML output updated to show prize pool and individual winnings
Rationale

The original TV show's prize money creates real stakes and clarifies the end-game mechanics. Players now understand the financial consequences of their decisions:

  • Faithful must be CERTAIN all traitors are gone before ending - one wrong call loses everything
  • Traitors have clear motivation to push for early game endings
  • The end-game vote becomes more strategic with explicit dollar amounts shown
Expected Impact

Should make faithful players more cautious about voting to end the game early. The explicit financial stakes may encourage more thorough investigation before the end-game vote, potentially improving faithful win rates.

v1.2.0 2025-12-07

Retrospective Analysis: Leverage Departed Player Information

Major overhaul to encourage players to analyze departed players' revealed roles and past behavior as crucial evidence, rather than ignoring them.

  • FAITHFUL Removed instructions telling players to "only discuss living players" and that "eliminated players are no longer relevant"
  • FAITHFUL Added strategic context in system prompt showing REVEALED TRAITORS, MURDERED faithful, and WRONGLY BANISHED faithful with analysis hints
  • FAITHFUL New voting history tracking for revealed traitors - shows who they voted for (likely faithful threats)
  • FAITHFUL Enhanced private thoughts prompt with specific analysis checklist:
    • Who defended/voted with revealed traitors?
    • What did murdered players suspect? (Their suspicions were likely correct)
    • Who pushed to banish faithful players?
    • Voting pattern analysis across multiple rounds
  • TRAITOR Added warning that faithful may analyze their alignment with revealed traitors
  • SYSTEM New _build_voting_history_context() method to track revealed traitors' voting patterns
Rationale

Previous prompts explicitly told players to ignore departed players: "Eliminated players are no longer relevant" and "Base your current suspicions on the LIVING players' behavior". This was strategically wrong because:

  • When players are eliminated, their role is REVEALED - this is crucial information
  • Revealed traitors' past behavior shows traitor tactics (who they defended, who they targeted)
  • Murdered players' suspicions were likely correct - traitors silence threats
  • Voting patterns with revealed traitors expose remaining traitor alliances
  • Who pushed to banish wrongly-eliminated faithful was likely a traitor
Expected Impact

Should significantly help faithful players by giving them tools to use revealed information strategically. Faithful now have explicit prompts to perform retrospective analysis that could expose traitor alliances based on historical behavior with revealed players.
